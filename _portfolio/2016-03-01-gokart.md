A fully functional **racing go-kart** built by a team of 20 mechanical engineering students from scratch.  
Participated in the **FMSCI National Racing Competition**, winning in the *Best Video of Journey in Making* category.  
Hands-on learning included **welding, machining, and fabrication techniques**.
