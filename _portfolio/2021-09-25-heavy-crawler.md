From the inspiration of NASA’s crawler, I designed and 3D-printed a mobile platform capable of carrying heavy loads while maintaining perfect straight-line motion.  
The system used **feedback loops** and **encoders** to synchronize motors, ensuring high positional accuracy and parallel motion.
