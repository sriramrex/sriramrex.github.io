This conference paper investigates the role of **artificial soft muscles** and soft actuation mechanisms in advancing humanoid robotics.  
It discusses a range of technologies — **pneumatic artificial muscles**, **electroactive polymers**, **nanotube actuators**, **shape-memory alloys**, and **hydraulically amplified electrostatic actuators (HASEL)** — highlighting their advantages in flexibility, energy efficiency, and biological inspiration.  
The work emphasizes how integrating such materials and actuators can enable humanoid robots with higher dexterity, adaptability, and natural motion compared to traditional rigid robotic systems.
